Blood Services

Ensuring a safe and adequate blood supply nationwide

Blood Collection

We operate 84 blood collection facilities and 13 blood centers nationwide, collecting over 500,000 units of blood annually. Our mobile blood donation units reach remote communities to ensure blood availability where it's needed most.

Blood Processing & Testing

All collected blood undergoes rigorous testing for HIV, Hepatitis B & C, Syphilis, and Malaria to ensure safety. We process blood into components (red cells, plasma, platelets) to maximize each donation's impact.

Blood Distribution

We supply blood to over 1,200 hospitals nationwide, ensuring that patients receive safe blood when they need it. Our distribution network includes refrigerated transport vehicles and cold chain management systems.

Disaster Management

Preparedness, response, and recovery for natural and man-made disasters

Disaster Preparedness

We conduct community-based disaster risk reduction training, establish early warning systems, and preposition relief items in disaster-prone areas. Our 143 volunteer emergency response teams are trained and ready to deploy within hours.

Emergency Response

During disasters, we provide search and rescue, emergency medical services, and relief distribution. Our fleet includes 178 ambulances, 6 rescue boats, and 2 humanitarian drones for rapid assessment and delivery of supplies.

Recovery & Rehabilitation

We support communities in rebuilding through shelter assistance, livelihood programs, and psychosocial support. Our cash assistance programs help families recover with dignity and choice.

Safety Services

Training and services to build safer communities

First Aid Training

We provide internationally recognized first aid training to individuals, communities, and organizations. Our courses include Basic Life Support, Advanced First Aid, and specialized training for workplaces and schools.

Water Safety

Our lifeguards patrol popular beaches during holidays and weekends, preventing drowning incidents. We also conduct swimming and water safety classes for children in flood-prone communities.

Road Safety

We partner with transportation companies to provide first aid training for drivers and conductors. Our volunteers provide first aid during traffic accidents and major events.

Community Health & Nursing

Improving health outcomes in vulnerable communities

Community-Based Health Programs

We run maternal and child health clinics, immunization drives, and nutrition programs in underserved areas. Our community health volunteers provide health education and basic services door-to-door.

HIV/AIDS Prevention

We provide counseling, testing, and support for people living with HIV. Our peer education programs reach key populations with prevention information and services.

Psychosocial Support

Trained volunteers provide psychological first aid to disaster survivors and vulnerable groups. We operate child-friendly spaces in evacuation centers and conduct stress debriefing sessions.

Youth & Volunteer Services

Empowering the next generation of humanitarians

Red Cross Youth

With over 1.2 million members in schools and communities, our youth programs develop leadership and humanitarian values. Activities include peer education, community service, and disaster preparedness training.

Volunteer Management

We recruit, train, and deploy volunteers across all our services. Our volunteers contribute over 5 million hours annually, equivalent to 2,500 full-time staff.

International Humanitarian Law

We promote respect for International Humanitarian Law through education and advocacy. Our programs target armed forces, police, universities, and the general public.
