Become a Red Cross Volunteer

Our volunteers are the heart of our humanitarian work. With over 500,000 volunteers nationwide, we're able to reach communities in need across the Philippines.

As a volunteer, you can contribute your time and skills in various areas: disaster response, blood services, health programs, safety training, youth activities, and administrative support.

Volunteer Opportunities

Find the role that matches your skills and interests

Disaster Response Volunteer

Join our emergency response teams during disasters. Training provided in first aid, search and rescue, and relief distribution.

  • Minimum commitment: 20 hours/month
  • Training: 40-hour basic disaster course
  • Physical requirement: Moderate
Apply Now

Health Services Volunteer

Support our health programs: blood donation drives, community clinics, health education, and immunization campaigns.

  • Medical background preferred but not required
  • Training: Basic health orientation
  • Flexible schedule available
Apply Now

Blood Services Volunteer

Assist at blood donation centers and mobile blood drives. Roles include donor screening, post-donation care, and blood drive organization.

  • Minimum commitment: 12 hours/month
  • Training: Blood services orientation
  • Ideal for students and professionals
Apply Now

Youth Volunteer

Join our Red Cross Youth program. Open to students aged 13-25. Develop leadership skills through humanitarian activities.

  • For students aged 13-25
  • School-based or community chapters
  • Leadership training provided
Apply Now

Training Instructor

Become a certified instructor for first aid, water safety, or disaster preparedness courses. Share life-saving skills with your community.

  • Requires prior certification
  • Training: Instructor course
  • Flexible teaching schedule
Apply Now

Administrative Support

Contribute your office skills: data entry, social media management, event coordination, or general office support at our chapters.

  • Office hours (9AM-5PM)
  • Remote options available
  • Various skill sets needed
Apply Now
